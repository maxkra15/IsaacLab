\ReportHeading{Executive summary}

\begin{executivesummarybox}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.25em, itemsep=0.15em, topsep=0pt, parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Throughput peaks at 2048 worlds} --- the optimal env count on a 32\,GiB RTX~5090:
        \textbf{ADMM 13{,}053 env-steps/s}, proxy 11{,}032, all CUDA-graph captured. 4096/8192 did not
        complete (build time + memory).
  \item \textbf{Proxy is faster at low counts, ADMM at high.} Proxy wins up to $\approx$\,1024 envs
        (cheaper coupling per env); ADMM's per-step scales better and overtakes it above that.
  \item \textbf{Startup is the cost of scale, not memory.} The hose is a thin Cosserat rod (no particle
        field), so memory stays low; but build/startup grows from $\approx$\,6\,s at 1 env to
        $\approx$\,7\,min at 2048. Per step the VBD/AVBD cable+contact solve dominates (42\,\%); the
        CUDA graph is essential ($\approx$\,6$\times$).
\end{itemize}
\end{executivesummarybox}

\section{Methodology}

Each point is one headless, kitless, CUDA-graph run on an idle NVIDIA GeForce RTX~5090, 40 warm-up + 80
measured steps, driven by the scripted grasp-insert policy (ten coupled substeps per step: MuJoCo-Warp
arm, VBD cable + hard AVBD contacts, coupling, plus the IK solve). The default collision config is
active for both backends: robot$\leftrightarrow$housing convex hulls, hose$\leftrightarrow$fridge-body
mesh, and plug$\leftrightarrow$socket. \textbf{Proxy} (\texttt{Isaac-Waterhose-Coupled-v0}) mirrors the
gripper as proxy bodies; \textbf{ADMM} (\texttt{Isaac-Waterhose-Admm-v0}) couples through an ADMM
contact constraint. 4096 and 8192 worlds did not complete within the run cap on this 32\,GiB GPU.

\section{Optimal world count}

\textbf{2048 worlds is the throughput optimum} on this 32\,GiB RTX~5090: env-steps/s climbs the whole
way (proxy 26$\to$11{,}032, ADMM 14$\to$13{,}053 from 1 to 2048), then 4096 and 8192 do not complete ---
build time and memory run out. \textbf{ADMM is the faster backend at the optimum} (13{,}053 vs.\
11{,}032), having overtaken proxy around 1024 worlds. If startup matters (it grows to $\approx$\,7\,min
at 2048), \textbf{1024 worlds is the sweet spot}: 8.9--9.3\,k env-steps/s at $\approx$\,2.5\,min build.
